📞 Call 112India's emergency response number
If someone is in immediate danger, has severe breathing trouble, collapses, has stroke symptoms, severe chest pain, or uncontrolled bleeding, call 112 now. Do not delay to browse this site.
Emergency first: Call 112 for a life-threatening emergency. Tell the operator your exact location, what happened, the person's condition, and when symptoms began. Follow their instructions. Official India 112 information.

      How to choose the next step

      1 · Mild and improvingRest, drink fluids when appropriate, monitor symptoms, and use only familiar self-care that is safe for your age and health conditions.
      2 · Persistent, worsening or uncertainContact a qualified doctor or local health facility. Seek advice sooner for infants, pregnancy, older adults, or people with chronic illness or weakened immunity.
      3 · Severe or life-threateningCall 112. Do not wait for a symptom guide or attempt to drive an unstable person yourself.
      Important safety limits: This educational prototype is not a diagnostic tool, telemedicine service, or substitute for a clinician. It does not know a person's age, pregnancy status, allergies, medicines, or medical history. Do not start, stop, or share prescription medicines based on this page. A qualified clinician should review every guide before public launch.
