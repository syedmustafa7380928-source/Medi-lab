About PulseLab

PulseLab is a health education and emergency navigation project designed to make understandable information more accessible in India.

It helps people recognize warning signs, take safer immediate steps, and locate medical services. It does not replace a qualified healthcare professional.

Important safety note

Medical information here is general education. Symptoms can have many causes. Seek professional care for medical concerns. For severe, sudden, or life-threatening symptoms, call 112.

Content should be reviewed by qualified clinicians before public or clinical use.
